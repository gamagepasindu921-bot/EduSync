Tuition Sector Challenges

Why Private Institutes
Need EduSync Automation

Replacing cash mismanagement and manual book-keeping with unified digital identity and atomic ledgers.

Traditional Problem

Cash Leakage & "Class Cuts"

Students receive physical money from parents but divert funds toward personal spending, resulting in uncollected fees and disputes.

EduSync Solution

Virtual wallet is locked for tuition fees; deducted automatically upon NFC card tap.

Traditional Problem

Entrance Queues & Manual Cards

Manual card marking and paper passbooks create morning gate bottlenecks, delaying lecture commencement and tiring administrative staff.

EduSync Solution

Sub-600ms Contactless NFC verification with instant smartphone QR code fallback.

Traditional Problem

Parental Visibility Void

Parents have no real-time way of knowing whether their children actually reached the hall safely or skipped class without authorization.

EduSync Solution

Automated 15-minute SMS Absent alerts and multi-child dashboard tracking.

Step-by-Step Architecture

How EduSync Works
From Parent Top-Up To Class Entry

A closed-loop, fraud-proof transaction process executed in sub-second speed.

01

Parent Tops Up Wallet

Parent logs into the portal and reloads the student's virtual wallet balance online via credit/debit card or counter deposit.

Online Gateway Top-Up
02

Student Taps NFC Card

Upon arriving at the institute entrance gate, the student taps their personal smart card on the contactless reader.

< 600ms Hardware Tap
03

Rules & Balance Check

Cloud server validates timetable schedule, checks wallet solvency, executes atomic deduction, and splits tutor revenue.

Atomic PostgreSQL TX
04

Records & SMS Sent

Gate green LED confirms entry. Attendance is marked in teacher ledger, and an automated SMS alerts parents instantly.

Tailored Benefits For Every
Ecosystem Participant

Engineered to satisfy institute leadership, tutors, parents, and students.

Institute Admins

  • Eliminate uncollected class fee arrears with upfront wallet balances.
  • Resolve entrance congestion through sub-second NFC card tap-in.

Tutors & Teachers

Students

  • Personal LMS dashboard with PDF past papers, notes, and quiz rewards.
  • Instant QR code fallback on phone if physical card is forgotten.
